True Graph Attention Network for CVD

Bipartite graph: 1500 patient nodes + 13 risk‑factor nodes (bmi_obese, hypertension, diabetes, smoking, etc.). Edges based on 13 binary flags. GATConv layers compute attention coefficients per edge. Final 8‑dim patient embeddings improve ensemble by capturing risk interactions. SHAP confirms 5 of top 10 features are GAT embeddings. Threshold 0.17 yields 91.78% sensitivity — crucial for early CVD detection.

Model saved as .pkl, dashboard uses calibrated coefficients to replicate attention and scores.
